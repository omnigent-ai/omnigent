# Search the board, spot stuck work, and comment on rendered files

- **Search a project's tickets.** A search box on the board finds tickets by any words in their title, description, labels or recent comments, newest first.
- **Stuck work stands out.** A card that nobody is working on, or that has waited too long to land or for a sign-off, turns amber and says how long. A "stalled" chip filters the board to those cards, and Wake the Lead nudges things along.
- **Trends on the Summary.** Small charts under the headline numbers show tickets filed and finished, open tickets, changes landed and spend, day by day.
- **Comment on a Markdown file as you read it.** Select text in a file's rendered preview to add a comment, and comments show up in every view of the file.
- **Hand a file's review to the team.** Address All now works on project files and sends all open comments to the right agent in one go, showing who addressed each one. Download also saves a project file instead of reopening it.
- **Let other projects' Leads read your board.** Owners can list projects whose Lead may read the board, read-only, and Rules shows how often each one has looked.
- **Start a role from scratch.** The Team dialog adds a Blank choice, and its role starters are now optional.
