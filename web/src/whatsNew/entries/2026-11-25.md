# Work survives restarts, plus Insights and live status on tickets

- **Restarting a computer's Omnigent host now resumes chats that were mid-task.** This works for both Claude-based and Codex-based chats. To stop a runaway agent, press Stop first; a restart alone won't stop it.
- **See how your projects are used.** A new Insights page across your projects, and an Insights section on each project's Summary, show how work starts, how the team is built and how quickly tickets ship.
- **Know when a change is live.** A ticket now says "Live in this app" once its change is running, or "Landed, waiting for a restart" until then.
- **Pull request status at a glance.** A ticket's pull request links show whether each one is open, merged or closed, plus its checks and any merge conflicts.
- **Names instead of emails.** The board shows people by first name, and an agent's comments read as its role "for" the person it works for.
- **Give each role its own KPIs,** set in the Team dialog and shown on the Summary. Owners can also edit the tools of a role's own copy of its agent.
- **Smoother tickets.** New ticket works from the Summary, the Rules page and over open dialogs, @mentions work in a new ticket's description, and decision buttons appear only for real choices.
- **A calmer What's New notice** now sits under the sidebar's main navigation, and you can reopen these notes from your profile menu.
- **Small touches.** An empty Inbox and empty board lanes now say what's going on, with optional playful touches you can turn off in Appearance. Phone layouts also got a round of fixes.
