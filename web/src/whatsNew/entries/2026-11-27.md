# Slack in your project, and questions that reach the right person

- **Talk to your project from Slack.** Link one Slack channel to a project from its Sources, and requests posted there go to the project's Lead, with its replies coming back to the same thread. You can pause, resume or unlink it any time.
- **Questions find the right person.** When an agent asks something on a ticket, it shows as "Question for you" to the person it's meant for, with the agent's recommendation and the full text of each choice. Sign-offs still owed are listed separately.
- **See what happened to your answer.** After you answer a ticket's question, the ticket shows whether your answer was delivered and picked up, and offers a Nudge if nobody responds within 10 minutes.
- **@mentions show who you mean.** A mention of a teammate in a ticket now appears as a chip with their avatar and name.
- **Easier-to-read Insights.** Insights panels now lead with big numbers and short captions, with the details one click away.
- **Simpler team editing.** Edit a team member's name right in the dialog's header. The "Owns / Doesn't own" lists are gone from the Team dialog.
- **Change saved workflows by asking.** An agent can now update a saved workflow in place, so schedules that run it keep working, or delete one when you ask.
