# A project Summary, clearer asks, and a team you can shape

- **See how a project is doing at a glance.** A new Summary tab shows the project's key numbers and KPI tiles with a 7-day trend. Click any number to see the tickets and changes behind it.
- **Decisions you owe stand out.** A ticket waiting on you opens with a "Decision needed" box at the top, and sign-offs can be approved with one click. On the board, a "Needs you" chip and a "My tickets" filter narrow it to your work, and the Inbox can be filtered to one project.
- **Ticket cards tell you what last happened,** including the latest comment meant for people, and the board now fits laptop screens.
- **Attach images and videos to tickets.** Images show as thumbnails and videos play inline. You can also mention teammates with @name in a comment to loop them in.
- **Shape your project's team.** Roles can start from a template and list what they own and don't own, you assign tickets by clicking an avatar, and a role's new chat opens with its charter and starter prompts. A new Manager role can invite and remove members.
- **One place for a project's rules.** A Rules page gathers how the project works, and the project's description and mandate now display as formatted text.
- **File tickets across shared projects,** straight onto another project's board.
- **Calmer restarts.** When Omnigent restarts, you see "Omnigent is restarting" instead of an error, the page recovers on its own, and messages sent during a long reply are no longer marked "Not delivered".
- **Tidier sidebar and phone layout.** Each project's sidebar entry has separate Agents and Sessions folders with a + to start a session, and the project header fits on phones.
